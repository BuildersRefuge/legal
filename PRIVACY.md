# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 5 October 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Builder's Refuge ("Builder's Refuge", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects personal data when you use our services.

This policy applies to:

- The Builder's Refuge Minecraft server (the "Minecraft Server")
- [buildersrefuge.com](https://buildersrefuge.com) (the "Main Site")
- [app.buildersrefuge.com](https://app.buildersrefuge.com) (the "App Site")
- The Builder's Refuge Discord server (the "Discord Server")
- [buildersrefuge.buycraft.net](https://buildersrefuge.buycraft.net) (the "Store")

By using our services, you acknowledge the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## Who We Are

Builder's Refuge is a Minecraft network focused on professional and creative Minecraft building. We provide services that allow players to create, collaborate and participate in the Builder's Refuge community.

Builder's Refuge is owned and operated by volunteers.

## Minimum Age

Builder's Refuge is intended for users aged 13 or over.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that personal data has been collected from a child under 13, we will take reasonable steps to remove that information.

Where applicable, users may also be required to meet a higher minimum age imposed by local law or by a third-party service used in connection with Builder's Refuge.

## Our Lawful Basis for Processing

Where applicable, we process personal data on the basis of our legitimate interests.

Our legitimate interests include operating and protecting the Builder's Refuge network, preventing misuse of our services, providing access to network features, synchronising Minecraft and Discord accounts where necessary, and improving the experience we provide to our users.

Where another lawful basis is required for a particular type of processing, we will process personal data in accordance with applicable data protection legislation.

## Information We Collect

When you access or use the Minecraft Server or Discord Server, we may collect and retain information including:

- Your Minecraft username and UUID
- Your Discord username and user ID
- IP addresses used to connect to the Minecraft Server within the previous three months
- IP addresses used to access the Main Site or App Site
- Your email address, where previously provided
- Your birthday month and day, where provided. We do not collect your birth year as part of this information
- Messages sent, edited or deleted within the Discord Server
- Information relating to the amount of time you spend on the Minecraft Server

Some of this information, such as usernames and identifiers, may be necessary for the operation of features that connect services across the Builder's Refuge network.

## Applications and Appeals

When submitting an application, appeal or other submission through the App Site, you may provide additional information, including:

- Images of your portfolio or previous work
- Notes, explanations or other information included with your submission

The information you provide will be used for the purpose of reviewing, administering and responding to your submission.

## Store Purchases

Our Store is provided and operated through Tebex, formerly known as Buycraft.

Tebex Limited operates the Store and acts as the Merchant of Record for purchases made through it. This means that Tebex processes payments and handles certain aspects of transactions, including payment processing, taxation, fraud prevention and chargebacks.

When you make a purchase through the Store, Tebex may collect and process information including your name, billing and contact information, Minecraft username or other account identifier, IP address, transaction information and payment-related information.

Builder's Refuge may receive information relating to purchases where this is necessary to fulfil your purchase, provide the relevant features or benefits, maintain transaction records, provide support, or prevent misuse of our services.

Payment information submitted directly to Tebex is processed in accordance with Tebex's own privacy practices. Builder's Refuge does not directly process your payment card details unless otherwise stated at the point of collection.

You should review the [Tebex Privacy Policy](https://checkout.tebex.io/privacy) for further information about how Tebex collects, uses, stores and shares personal data.

## Website Forms

If you submit information through a form on one of our websites, the information you enter may be stored and used for the purpose described by that form.

This may include information submitted through applications, appeals, support requests or other features made available through our websites.

## How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

- Operate and maintain the Builder's Refuge network and its associated services
- Provide access to Discord features that require a corresponding Minecraft rank or account
- Provide access to features available through the Minecraft Server
- Link or synchronise information between Minecraft and Discord where required
- Prevent, identify and investigate misuse, abuse or unauthorised use of our services
- Moderate and protect the Builder's Refuge community
- Review applications, appeals and other submissions
- Inform you of the outcome of submissions made through the App Site
- Provide support and respond to enquiries
- Maintain the security and integrity of our services

We will not use personal data for purposes that are incompatible with those for which it was originally collected unless permitted or required by law.

## Third-Party Services

Builder's Refuge uses third-party services to provide certain functionality across our network. These services may process information independently and may have their own privacy policies, terms of service and data-processing practices.

One such service is [MinecraftAuth.me](https://minecraftauth.me), which may be used to link or verify Minecraft accounts with supported third-party platforms.

MinecraftAuth.me is a third-party service operated independently from Builder's Refuge. The owner of MinecraftAuth.me is also a member of the Builder's Refuge administration team. MinecraftAuth.me maintains its own privacy and legal policies, which are available at:

[https://github.com/MinecraftAuthentication/legal](https://github.com/MinecraftAuthentication/legal)

Your use of MinecraftAuth.me may therefore also be subject to MinecraftAuth.me's own terms and privacy policies.

As mentioned in Store Purchases, we use Tebex for our donation store. Please review [Tebex's Privacy Policy](https://checkout.tebex.io/privacy).

Other third-party services used by Builder's Refuge may include Discord, payment providers, hosting providers and other infrastructure or platform providers required to operate our services.

## Embedded and Third-Party Content

Our websites may contain content or services provided by third parties, including embedded videos, images, applications, payment services or other external content.

When you interact with embedded or third-party content, the relevant third party may collect information about you in the same way as if you had visited its website or service directly.

This may include the use of cookies or similar technologies, collection of technical information, and monitoring of your interaction with that content. Where applicable, the collection and use of this information is governed by the third party's own privacy policy.

## Information Sharing

We may disclose information where it is reasonably necessary to operate our services, protect the Builder's Refuge network, comply with a legal obligation, or work with service providers that support the operation of our infrastructure.

Information may also be processed by third-party services where you interact with those services through Builder's Refuge, including services such as Discord, MinecraftAuth.me and the Store.

We do not sell personal data to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

## International Data Processing

Builder's Refuge is accessible internationally, and some of the third-party services we use may operate or process information in countries other than the country in which you reside.

In particular, some services may process or store information in the United States. This may include third-party services such as MinecraftAuth.me or other infrastructure and platform providers used by Builder's Refuge.

As a result, information provided through Builder's Refuge may be transferred to, stored in or processed in the United States or other jurisdictions.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used when personal data is transferred internationally.

By using third-party services connected to Builder's Refuge, you should also review the privacy policies of those services for further information about where and how they process personal data.

## Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for the continued operation and security of our services, or where we are required to retain it for legal, administrative or security purposes.

Certain information has more specific retention periods. For example, IP addresses used to connect to the Minecraft Server are retained for up to three months.

Information submitted through forms may be retained for as long as it remains necessary for the purpose associated with the relevant form.

Where information is no longer required and there is no lawful reason for us to retain it, it may be deleted or anonymised.

## Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are also responsible for maintaining the security of their own Minecraft, Discord and other third-party accounts.

## Your Rights

Depending on the data protection laws applicable to you, you may have the right to:

- Request access to the personal data we hold about you
- Request a copy of personal data you have provided to us
- Request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
- Request the deletion of your personal data
- Object to the processing of your personal data
- Request that the processing of your personal data be restricted

These rights may be subject to exceptions where we are required or permitted to retain or process information for legal, administrative, security or other legitimate purposes.

To exercise your rights or make an enquiry regarding your personal data, you can:

- Open a support ticket in the `#support-tickets` channel on the [Builder's Refuge Discord Server](https://discord.gg/steak)
- Email [legal@buildersrefuge.com](mailto:legal@buildersrefuge.com)

You also have the right to make a complaint to the relevant data protection or supervisory authority in your jurisdiction.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, our data-processing practices or applicable legal requirements.

The effective date displayed at the top of this policy will be updated when changes are made. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically to remain informed about how we handle personal data.
