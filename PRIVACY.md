# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 5 October 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Builder's Refuge ("Builder's Refuge", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects personal data when you use our services.

This policy applies to:

- The Builder's Refuge Minecraft server (the "Minecraft Server")
- [buildersrefuge.com](https://buildersrefuge.com) (the "Main Site")
- [app.buildersrefuge.com](https://app.buildersrefuge.com) (the "App Site")
- [map.buildersrefuge.com](https://map.buildersrefuge.com) (the "Map Site")
- The Builder's Refuge Discord server (the "Discord Server")
- [buildersrefuge.buycraft.net](https://buildersrefuge.buycraft.net) (the "Store")

This policy is provided for transparency. It explains what we do with your data; it is not a request for your consent unless we say otherwise below.

## Who We Are

Builder's Refuge is a Minecraft network focused on professional and creative Minecraft building. We provide services that allow players to create, collaborate and take part in the Builder's Refuge community.

Builder's Refuge is run by a volunteer administration team. For the purposes of data protection law, the data controller can be contacted at [legal@buildersrefuge.com](mailto:legal@buildersrefuge.com).

## Minimum Age

Builder's Refuge is intended for users aged 13 or over.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will delete it, including any application or appeal they have submitted.

You may need to meet a higher minimum age set by the law where you live or by a third-party service used with Builder's Refuge, such as Discord.

## Information We Collect

### Account and profile information

- Your Minecraft username, UUID and previous usernames
- Your in-game nickname, if you set one
- Your Discord user ID, username and avatar, if you link your Discord account
- Your birthday (month and day only, never the year), if you provide it
- Your timezone. This is detected automatically from your IP address when you connect, and you can change it
- Your in-game preferences and settings, such as chat, teleport, party and sound settings
- Your playtime and the last time you were seen online
- Your ranks and permission groups

### Connection information

Each time you connect to the Minecraft Server, we record:

- Your IP address
- The hostname and internet service provider (ISP) associated with that IP address
- An approximate location (country/region/city) derived from your IP address
- Your client language (locale) and the name of the Minecraft client you use
- Your connection latency (ping)
- The times you joined and left

Location and ISP information is looked up **locally** using the MaxMind GeoLite2 database. Your IP address is not sent to a third party for this lookup.

We compare connection records to identify accounts that have connected from the same IP address. We use this to detect ban evasion and abuse of our services (see "Automated Processing" below).

When you use the App Site or Main Site, our web servers record your IP address, the pages you request, and (if you are signed in) your Minecraft username.

### Chat and communications

- **In-game chat.** Messages sent in chat on the Minecraft Server are recorded in our server logs, which staff can access. Messages sent in the global chat channel are relayed to a channel in the Discord Server, where they are visible to its members.
- **Private messages.** Staff may view private messages sent between players on the Minecraft Server, to moderate the community and investigate reports.
- **Discord.** Messages sent, edited or deleted within the Discord Server are logged for moderation purposes.

### Gameplay information

- Homes you set, your logout location and your teleport history
- Friends, ignored players and parties
- Worlds and plots you own or have access to, including their names, settings, trusted players and builds
- Snapshots (backups) of your worlds and plots
- Map share links you create and custom world generators you save

### Applications and appeals

When you submit a membership application through the App Site, we collect:

- Your Discord user ID and username
- Your email address
- Your portfolio link and any images you upload
- The information you write in your application
- Your total playtime at the time you apply

When you submit a ban appeal, we collect your Discord user ID and username, details of the ban being appealed, and the statement you write.

When you submit a portfolio link, our systems may automatically visit that page to retrieve a preview for reviewers.

Staff may add internal comments to applications and appeals. We also record the decision, who reviewed it and when, and (for appeals) the reason for any denial and when you may appeal again.

### Moderation records

If you receive a ban, mute, kick or warning, we keep a record of it, including the reason, who issued it, when it was issued and when it expires.

### Store purchases

Our Store is provided and operated by Tebex Limited, formerly known as Buycraft, which acts as the Merchant of Record. Tebex processes payments and handles payment processing, taxation, fraud prevention and chargebacks.

When you make a purchase, Tebex may collect your name, billing and contact information, Minecraft username, IP address, and transaction and payment information. We receive purchase information from Tebex where it is needed to deliver your purchase, keep transaction records, provide support or prevent misuse. We never receive your full payment card details.

Please review the [Tebex Privacy Policy](https://checkout.tebex.io/privacy) for information about how Tebex handles your data.

## Cookies

The App Site and Map Site use only cookies that are strictly necessary for them to work:

- **Authentication cookie** (`jwt`): keeps you signed in. It is shared across buildersrefuge.com subdomains.
- **Map share cookies** (`br_map_shares`, `br_map_unlocks`): remember map share links you have opened and password-protected maps you have unlocked.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Operate and maintain the Builder's Refuge network and its services
- Provide features on the Minecraft Server, App Site and Map Site, such as worlds, plots, homes, friends and map sharing
- Link your Minecraft and Discord accounts and give you the matching Discord roles
- Review applications and appeals and tell you the outcome, including by Discord direct message
- Moderate the community, investigate reports, and prevent misuse, abuse and ban evasion
- Provide support and respond to enquiries
- Fulfil Store purchases
- Maintain the security and integrity of our services

We will not use personal data for purposes incompatible with those for which it was collected, unless permitted or required by law.

## Our Lawful Basis for Processing

Where data protection laws such as the UK GDPR or EU GDPR apply, we rely on the following lawful bases:

- **Legitimate interests:** operating, securing and moderating the network, providing its features, linking accounts, detecting ban evasion, and reviewing applications and appeals. We have balanced these interests against your rights, and you may object to this processing (see "Your Rights").
- **Contract:** delivering Store purchases.
- **Consent:** optional information you choose to give us, such as your birthday, nickname, portfolio images and email address. You can withdraw consent at any time by removing the information or contacting us.
- **Legal obligation:** keeping records where the law requires it, for example transaction records.

## Automated Processing

We automatically flag Minecraft accounts that have connected from the same IP address as each other. When a flagged account is linked to a banned account, staff are notified in-game and in a staff-only Discord channel.

These flags do not lead to decisions being made about you automatically. Staff review them before taking any action.

## Staff Access

Members of our staff team can access personal data where they need it for their role, for example to moderate, review applications and appeals, or provide support.

Administrators can view the App Site as a specific user to diagnose problems and provide support. They cannot do this to delete your data on your behalf.

## Information Visible to Others

Some information is visible to other players or to the public by design:

- Your username, nickname, player head and rank
- Messages you send in public chat, including messages relayed to the Discord Server
- Worlds you own, their names and your trusted players, to players who can access those worlds
- Maps of worlds you have made public, or shared using a map share link. Anyone who has the link can view them, until it expires or you revoke it

## Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to provide certain features. These services operate independently and have their own privacy policies.

- **Discord:** community platform, account linking, notifications and staff channels.
- **[MinecraftAuth.me](https://minecraftauth.me):** used to sign in to the App Site and link or verify Minecraft accounts. MinecraftAuth.me is operated independently from Builder's Refuge, although its owner is also a member of the Builder's Refuge administration team. Its policies are available at [github.com/MinecraftAuthentication/legal](https://github.com/MinecraftAuthentication/legal).
- **Tebex:** our Store (see "Store purchases").

When you use the App Site, your browser loads content directly from the following services. These receive your IP address and, where noted, other information:

- **heads.discordsrv.com:** player head images (receives Minecraft UUIDs)
- **Discord CDN** (cdn.discordapp.com): Discord avatars
- **assets.mcasset.cloud:** Minecraft textures and icons

## Embedded and Third-Party Content

Our websites may contain embedded content from third parties, such as videos and images. When you view or interact with that content, the third party may collect information about you, including through cookies, as if you had visited its website directly. That collection is governed by the third party's own privacy policy.

## Information Sharing

We share personal data only:

- With the third-party services described above, to the extent needed for them to provide their service
- Where reasonably necessary to protect the Builder's Refuge network, our users or the public
- Where required by law, such as in response to a valid request from law enforcement

**We do not sell personal data.**

## International Data Processing

Our servers are located in the United States. Some of the third-party services we use, including Discord, MinecraftAuth.me and Tebex, may store or process information in the United States or other countries.

Where personal data is transferred internationally, we rely on the transfer safeguards offered by those providers, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, where available.

## Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy. Specific retention periods are:

| Data                                                                         | Retention                                                                                                         |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Minecraft connection records (IP address, ISP, location, client information) | Deleted after 3 months, except the most recent connection for each username, which is kept to prevent ban evasion |
| World and plot snapshots                                                     | 30 days                                                                                                           |
| Archived worlds                                                              | Permanently deleted 30 days after archiving                                                                       |
| Map share links                                                              | Until they expire or you revoke them                                                                              |
| Server logs, including in-game chat                                          | 90 days                                                                                                           |
| Discord moderation logs                                                      | Indefinitely                                                                                                      |
| Applications and appeals                                                     | for as long as your account exists                                                                                |
| Moderation records (bans, mutes, kicks, warnings)                            | indefinitely, to enforce bans and prevent ban evasion                                                             |
| Account, profile and gameplay data                                           | Until you delete it, or until it is no longer needed                                                              |
| Store transaction records                                                    | as long as required for tax and accounting purposes                                                               |

When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

## Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse. Access to personal data is limited to staff who need it for their role.

No method of sending or storing information electronically is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your Minecraft, Discord and other accounts secure.

## Your Rights

Depending on the data protection laws that apply to you, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Receive a copy of your data in a portable format
- Have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected
- Have your data deleted
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- Have the processing of your data restricted
- Withdraw consent where we rely on consent

### Self-service

You can view, download (as JSON) and delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. Use the **"Your data"** link at the bottom of [app.buildersrefuge.com](https://app.buildersrefuge.com).

When you delete your data, we remove your profile information, settings, worlds, plots, homes, friends, applications, appeals and ranks. We keep the following, for the reasons given:

- **Moderation records,** to enforce bans and prevent ban evasion
- **Connection records,** under the retention period above, to prevent ban evasion
- **Your Minecraft UUID and username,** which those records are linked to
- **Entries on other players' world deny lists,** which are decisions made by those players

Staff accounts cannot use self-service deletion and should contact us instead.

### Contacting us

To exercise your rights, or for any other privacy enquiry, you can:

- Open a support ticket in the `#support-tickets` channel on the [Builder's Refuge Discord Server](https://discord.gg/steak)
- Email [legal@buildersrefuge.com](mailto:legal@buildersrefuge.com)

We will respond within one month. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request, for example by asking you to sign in with your Minecraft account.

You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country, such as the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK.
